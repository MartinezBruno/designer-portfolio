## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 7 goal (verbatim): "Moving through the site feels fluid: animated page transitions, inertial scrolling, and parallax. Keyboard users, screen-reader users, and find-in-page all keep working, and animations never leak between pages."

<objective>
Vertical slice 2: the visitor now SEES the transition. Under `on` an ink panel sweeps across the content area while the header stays still; under `reduced` the pages crossfade. Built as native same-document View Transition CSS on top of the 07-01 router.

Purpose: MOTN-05 "animated page transitions" (D-01, D-03 wipe on EN/ES, D-04 reduced crossfade, D-05 header still). The entrance hold and focus timing come in 07-04; the title morph in 07-06.
Output: three motion tokens, the panel element, header VT hook, VT CSS, `data-wipe` handling in navigation.ts, e2e assertions, and an opt-in screenshot capture spec reviewed with the taste skill.
</objective>
