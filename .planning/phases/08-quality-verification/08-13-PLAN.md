Pitfall [Based on CSS border-radius scaling rules]: `rounded-t-full` plus `rounded-b-[1.25rem]` does not produce the arch. When adjacent radii exceed a side, the browser scales all radii by one factor, which shrinks the 1.25rem bottom corners to almost 0.
