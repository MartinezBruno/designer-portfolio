## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 6 goal (verbatim): "A visitor feels Martina's design sensibility through scroll reveals, headline text animations, a playful cursor, magnetic buttons, and image hover effects. Anyone who prefers less motion gets a calm, fully usable site."

<objective>
Vertical slice for "anyone who prefers less motion": the motion gate gains a third state (`on` / `reduced` / `off`), OS reduced motion resolves to `reduced`, and a visitor can press a header toggle to reduce motion live, persisted pre-paint across pages. Every later Phase 6 effect reads `data-motion` and listens to the `motion:change` event this plan creates.

Purpose: MOTN-07 and MOTN-08 (D-14..D-18). Supersedes Phase 1's "OS reduce resolves to off" behaviour per D-14.
Output: extended `gate.ts`, new `events.ts`, `toggle.ts`, `MotionToggle.astro`, header placement, three dictionary keys (incl. `cursor.view` for plan 06-09, so the dictionaries are touched once), updated unit/e2e tests.
</objective>
