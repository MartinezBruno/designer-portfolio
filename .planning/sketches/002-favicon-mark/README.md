# Sketch 002: Favicon mark

## Design Question
The site still ships Astro's default favicon. Which mark reads at 16 px in a browser tab (light and dark),
holds up as a 180 px iPhone icon, and belongs to the site's language (accent blue #0f70b7, Bricolage
Grotesque display, the arch portrait from sketch 001)?

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/002-favicon-mark/index.html

## Variants
- **A: Arco**: the hero arch silhouette, solid blue, no letter.
- **B: Arco + M**: blue arch with a white geometric M (stroked).
- **C: m en Bricolage**: white Bricolage "m" on a blue rounded square.
- **D: MF monograma**: black MF on white with an ink border.
- **★ E: m. con punto azul (winner)**: black Bricolage "m" with a blue full stop, white disc.
- **F: Arco blanco sobre azul**: blue rounded square with the arch cut out in white.

## What to Look For
- Recognition among other tabs at real 16 px, light and dark Chrome
- Pixel legibility at 16 px (×5 zoom): letters (C, D, E) vs shapes (A, B, F)
- The 180 px iPhone tile: full-bleed square, iOS rounds the corners
- Production would ship SVG paths (glyphs outlined), favicon.ico 32, apple-touch-icon 180, 192/512 manifest icons
