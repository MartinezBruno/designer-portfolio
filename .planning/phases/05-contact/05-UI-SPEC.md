# Phase 5 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**What this contract covers:** the visitor-facing parts of Phase 5, in EN and ES:
- the new Contact section at the end of Home (`/#contact`, `/es/#contact`): the invitation column, the form, and the no-JS mailto block (CONT-01, CONT-05)
- every form state: initial, pre-selected, invalid, sending, failed, sent (CONT-01, CONT-03)
- the visible side of the spam guard: the honeypot (invisible) and the Turnstile widget (CONT-02)
- the "Ask about this" link on each service card, and the pre-selection it triggers (HOME-08)
- re-pointing every "Contact" link to Home's section (D-04)

**Out of scope:**
- the mailer, the email body and the server internals (CONT-04). They have no UI. Only the visitor-facing result of a send (success or failure) is specified here.
- motion beyond the existing `motion-safe:` colour transitions and one spinner (Phases 6–7)
- deployment, real delivery, auto-reply, rate limiting, extra fields (CONTEXT Deferred)

**Inheritance:** `01-UI-SPEC.md`, `02-UI-SPEC.md`, `03-UI-SPEC.md` and `04-UI-SPEC.md` (all approved) still apply where this document is silent. That covers tokens, the accent focus ring, 44px touch targets, the `ButtonLink` pill, the text-link pattern, "no content hidden by `data-motion`", the copy rules, and the rule of no string literals in components.

**Provenance labels:**
- `[CONTEXT D-nn]`: a locked decision in `05-CONTEXT.md`.
- `[Research]`: a finding in `05-RESEARCH.md` (its own label is kept where it matters, e.g. `[Research, VERIFIED]`).
- `[Upstream]`: decided in an earlier UI-SPEC, PROJECT/CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md. The source is named.
- `[Codebase]`: read from `src/` on 2026-10-20.
- `[Verified]`: measured or computed on 2026-10-20. The method is named.
- `[Default]`: my choice, inside a CONTEXT "Claude's Discretion" area.
- `[Inference]` / `[Assumption]`: not yet measured or confirmed. Each one says how it will be checked.

**Taste skill:** CLAUDE.md requires `taste-skill` for this work. The skill's special case "A component or a single section, not a page" applies: the Contact section and the card link sit inside an established design with locked tokens and type. No direction is drawn. The surrounding design is matched and the house rules and laws are applied. Decisions taken from the skill are tagged `[Taste: <rule>]`. The result must pass `lint.mjs` and `inspect.mjs`, and the screenshots (including the form states) must be reviewed (see Verification).

**Visual hierarchy (Contact section):**
1. **Focal point: the section heading** "Tell me about your project", at 36px. It is the largest type below the hero and marks the page's closing scene. `[Taste: get the big thing big and the small things small]`
2. **Second: the form's one action,** the accent "Send message" pill. It is the section's only accent fill. `[Taste: the accent appears on the action and perhaps one other thing]`
3. **Third: the service chips.** When a visitor arrives from a card, the checked chip is the one ink-filled shape in the form. `[CONTEXT specifics: "the pre-selected service obvious the moment the visitor lands"]`
4. Fields, the email link and socials stay quiet: ink on cream, filled fields, no boxes.

## Spacing Scale

Same 4px-based Tailwind scale as Phases 1–4. No new spacing tokens.

| Token | Value | Tailwind | Usage (Phase 5) |
|-------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| xs | 4px | `1` | Email link icon-to-text gap (`gap-1`, same as the footer) |
| sm | 8px | `2` | Label → hint → field → error stack inside one field (`gap-2`); gap between chips (`gap-2`); icon-to-text in chips, errors and links (`gap-2`); email link to socials row (`gap-2`) |
| md | 16px | `4` | Chip side padding (`px-4`); field side padding (`px-4`); legend to chips (`mb-4` on the legend) |
| lg | 24px | `6` | Left-column stack (`gap-6`); name/email column gap (`sm:gap-x-6`); Turnstile-to-button gap (`gap-6`); success panel padding on phones (`p-6`) and its stack (`gap-6`) |
| xl | 32px | `8` | Gap between form groups: summary, chips, name/email row, message, submit row, failure block (`gap-8`); success panel padding from `sm` (`sm:p-8`); grid column gap at `lg` (`lg:gap-x-8`) |
| 2xl | 48px | `12` | Gap between the stacked columns below `lg` (`gap-y-12`) |
| 3xl | 64px | `16` | Section rhythm: the section is the last child of Home's existing `flex flex-col gap-16 py-16` wrapper, so it sits 64px below the tools row and 64px above the footer `[Codebase: HomeView]` |

Exceptions:
- **Touch targets:** chips `min-h-11` (44px); text inputs `h-12` (48px); buttons `h-12` (48px, as `ButtonLink`); every text link `min-h-11`. `[Upstream: 01-UI-SPEC]`
- **Textarea:** `min-h-40` (160px), `resize-y`. A multiple of 4, a height floor, not a spacing step.
- **Turnstile slot:** `min-h-[140px] w-[150px]`. These are Cloudflare's fixed dimensions for the compact widget `[Research: CITED, 150×140]`. The slot is reserved before the script loads, so nothing shifts when the widget appears. Not a spacing token.
- **Anchor offset:** `scroll-mt-24` (96px) on the section. The sticky header is 64px from `sm` and two 44px rows (88px) below `sm` `[Codebase: SiteHeader]`, so 96px keeps the heading clear at every width.
- **Honeypot:** positioned off-screen with `absolute -left-[9999px]`. Not layout. (Never `h-px`/`w-px`: `lint.mjs` reads those as hairlines.)
- Text measure: the invitation paragraph and the no-JS paragraph are `max-w-[60ch]` `[Taste: 45–75 characters per line]`.
- **Known, not changed here:** section gaps stay at 64px. Taste asks for 120–220px. Inherited Open Question (Phase 3 OQ 4).

## Typography

Phase 5 declares **exactly 4 sizes: 14, 16, 24 and 36** (the same set as Phase 4, all already compiled) and **2 weights: 400 and 600**.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Font |
|------|------|--------|-------------|------|
| Label / meta | 14px (`text-sm`) | 400 for hints, field errors and the Turnstile notes; 600 for chip labels and button labels (as `ButtonLink`) | 1.4 | Inter |
| Body | 16px (`text-base`) | 400 for the invitation, input text, success body, failure body; 600 for field labels, the legend, the summary and failure titles, and text links | 1.5 | Inter |
| Heading | 24px (`text-2xl`) | 600 | 1.3 | Bricolage Grotesque |
| Display | 36px (`text-4xl`) | 600 | 1.1 | Bricolage Grotesque |

**Why 36 for the section h2:** the other Home section headings are 24px. Contact is the page's closing scene and its one conversion point, so its heading steps up to the size Phase 4 already uses for detail titles. It adds no token, and Home stays well under inspect's size ceiling (9). `[Taste: two or three sizes do the visible work; Upstream: 04-UI-SPEC text-4xl]` `[Inference]` "Tell me about your project" (26 characters) fits in 2 lines at 36px in a 288px column; Phase 4 measured "Need something like this?" (25 characters) at 2 lines there. Gated by inspect's `headline-lines` at 390 and the e2e line count below.

### Phase 5 usage map

| Element | Tag | Classes (contract) |
|---------|-----|--------------------|
| Section heading | `<h2 id="contact-title">` | `font-display text-4xl font-semibold tracking-tight text-ink text-pretty` |

| Field label | `<label for>` | `text-base font-semibold text-ink` |
| Hint | `<p id="…-hint">` | `text-sm text-ink-muted` |
| Input / textarea text | `<input>` / `<textarea>` | `text-base text-ink` (16px also prevents iOS zoom on focus) |
| Field error | `<p id="…-error">` | `text-sm text-danger inline-flex items-start gap-2` |
| Summary / failure title | `<p>` | `text-base font-semibold text-danger inline-flex items-center gap-2` |
| Success heading | `<h3 tabindex="-1">` | `font-display text-2xl font-semibold tracking-tight text-ink` |
| Success / failure / no-JS body | `<p>` | `text-base text-ink max-w-[60ch] break-words` |
| Turnstile notes | `<p>` | `text-sm text-ink-muted` (loading, waiting) / `text-sm text-danger` (load error) |

Rules:
- **Heading outline on Home:** h1 hero → h2 roles (sr-only) → h2 services → h3 ×4 → h2 latest work → h3 tiles → h2 tools → **h2 Contact** → (**h3 "Message sent"** only while the success panel shows) → h2 footer.
- **User text never becomes a heading.** The visitor's name appears only inside the success body paragraph, never in a heading, so a 100-character name cannot break the two-line rule. `[Taste: headlines two lines or fewer; Default]` That paragraph has `break-words` so a long unbroken name cannot overflow at 320px.
- No placeholders inside fields. Labels are always visible, and hints sit above the field. `[Default; Based on patterns: placeholder-as-label fails once text is typed]`
- No `truncate`, `line-clamp`, fixed text heights, uppercase transforms or letter-spaced labels. `[Taste: no tiny uppercase letter-spaced labels]`

### Section frame

```
<section id="contact" aria-labelledby="contact-title"
         class="mx-auto w-full max-w-6xl scroll-mt-24 px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8">
  <div class="grid gap-y-12 lg:grid-cols-12 lg:gap-x-8">
    <div class="flex flex-col items-start gap-6 lg:col-span-5">     ← invitation column
      <h2 id="contact-title">{contact.heading}</h2>
      <p>{contact.intro}</p>
      <div class="flex flex-col items-start gap-2">
        <p><a href="mailto:{site.email}">{mail icon} {site.email}</a></p>
        <ul class="flex gap-2"> {IconLink per configured social, same as the footer} </ul>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="lg:col-span-7">                                       ← form column
      <div data-contact-nojs> … </div>            visible in the HTML (CONT-05)
      <form data-contact-form hidden novalidate aria-labelledby="contact-title"> … </form>
      <div data-contact-success hidden> … </div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p role="status" class="sr-only" data-contact-status></p>          ← the one live region, outside the form
</section>
```

- **Two columns from `lg` (1024px), stacked below** `[CONTEXT D-02]`. 5/12 invitation, 7/12 form: the form gets the wider column because it carries the content. Stacked order = DOM order = invitation, then form.
- The invitation column holds the email link and socials **with the same markup as the footer** (mail icon + address; `IconLink` per configured social). Extract the socials list into `src/components/ui/SocialLinks.astro` and use it in both places. `[Default; Upstream: AGENTS.md no duplicated classes]`
- **No box around the form, no panel behind the section.** The form sits on the page ground. Its fields are the only filled shapes. `[Taste: no outlined boxes; one tone step]`
- The status region lives outside the form, so it survives when the form is hidden on success. It exists in the HTML from page load (live regions must exist before their content changes). `[Research: Pattern 5]`
- Data hooks (`data-contact-*`) are the contract between markup, script and e2e. The planner may rename them, but must keep one hook per state block.

### No-JS block (CONT-05, D-12)

```
<div data-contact-nojs class="flex flex-col items-start gap-6">
  <p class="max-w-[60ch] text-base text-ink">{contact.nojs.body → "Email me at {email} …"}</p>
  <ButtonLink href="mailto:{site.email}?subject={encodeURIComponent(contact.nojs.subject)}">
    {contact.nojs.button}<arrow-right slot="icon"/>
  </ButtonLink>
</div>
```

- Shipped **visible**. The contact script hides it (`hidden = true`) in the same task that reveals the form (`hidden = false`). If the script never runs, the visitor keeps a working mailto and never types a message they can't send. No `<noscript>`. `[CONTEXT D-12; Research: Pattern 6, same idiom as EmbedFacade]`
- The copy is neutral ("Email me at …"), not "this form needs JavaScript". So the brief moment before the script swaps the blocks reads naturally for JS visitors too. `[Default]` `[Inference]` The swap happens below the fold for nearly every visit (the section is the last on Home); when landing directly on `#contact`, the swap stays inside the section, under its heading, so the heading does not move.
- The address in the paragraph is plain text (the button is the link). The invitation column's email link is also present. `[Default]`

### Form layout (JS on)

DOM order, top to bottom (`<form class="flex flex-col gap-8">`):

1. **Error summary** (`data-contact-summary`, `hidden` until a failed validation)
2. **Service chips** (`<fieldset>`)
3. **Name + email** row: `grid gap-8 sm:grid-cols-2 sm:gap-x-6`
4. **Message**
5. **Honeypot** (off-screen, see Spam guard)
6. Hidden `<input type="hidden" name="locale" value={locale}>`
7. **Submit row:** `flex flex-col items-start gap-6 sm:flex-row sm:items-end`, holding the Turnstile slot then the submit button
8. **Failure block** (`data-contact-failure`, `hidden` until a failed send)

Pre-selected chips come first, so a visitor arriving from a card sees the chosen service at the top of the form before anything else. `[CONTEXT specifics; Default]`

### Service chips (`ServiceChips.astro`) `[CONTEXT D-06, D-07]`

```
<fieldset class="m-0 min-w-0 border-0 p-0">          ← not flex: a legend is never a flex item
  <legend class="mb-4 …">{contact.service.legend} <span class="font-normal">{contact.optional}</span></legend>
  <div class="flex flex-wrap gap-2">
    {choices.map(c =>
      <div>
        <input type="radio" name="service" id="contact-service-{c.id}" value={c.id}
               checked={c.id === 'other'} class="peer sr-only" />
        <label for="contact-service-{c.id}" class={chip}>
          <IconCheck aria-hidden focusable="false" class="size-4" /> {c.label}
        </label>
      </div>)}
  </div>
</fieldset>
```

- **Options, in order:** the 4 services in Phase 2 order (Graphic design, Web design, Branding, Social media), labelled from the localized `services.json` titles, then **"Not sure yet / Something else"** with value `other`. `other` is checked in the HTML, so the default holds with no script. `[CONTEXT D-07; Codebase: services.json]` A unit test asserts that the chip ids equal the `services.json` ids plus `other` (drift guard). `[Research: Pattern 3]`
- `chip` class constant (defined once in the component, with the comment `// taste-skill-ignore: 2px radio-chip boundary (WCAG 1.4.11), not a 1px box` on the same line so `lint.mjs` doesn't flag `outlined-box`):
  `inline-flex min-h-11 items-center gap-2 rounded-full border-2 border-ink-muted px-4 py-2 text-left text-sm font-semibold text-ink cursor-pointer hover:border-ink [&>svg]:hidden peer-checked:border-ink peer-checked:bg-ink peer-checked:text-surface peer-checked:[&>svg]:block peer-focus-visible:outline-2 peer-focus-visible:outline-offset-2 peer-focus-visible:outline-accent motion-safe:transition-colors motion-safe:duration-150`
- **Rest:** transparent fill, 2px ink-muted border, ink label. **Checked:** ink fill, surface label, check icon shown. The state is carried by fill **and** the icon shape, never colour alone.
- **Shape:** fully round, the same as every pressable control on the site. Fields, the places you write, are square. One rule, decided once: pressable = round, writable = square. `[Taste: buttons square or fully round, decided once]`
- Labels may wrap inside the pill (`py-2`, no `whitespace-nowrap`), so the longest label ("Not sure yet / Something else") can never overflow at 320px. `[Inference]` About 276px at 320 wide on one line, so it is close; the e2e overflow check is the gate.
- **Keyboard:** native radio group. One Tab stop (the checked chip); arrow keys move and check. The focus ring is drawn on the label through `peer-focus-visible`. `[Research: Pattern 5]` `[Inference]` Tailwind 4 compiles the stacked `peer-checked:[&>svg]:block` variant; confirm in the built CSS. If it doesn't, put `group/chip` on the label's wrapper and use `group-has-checked/chip:` instead.

### Text fields

Each field: `<div class="flex flex-col gap-2">` → `<label for>` → hint `<p>` (message only) → control → error `<p hidden>`.

| Field | Control | Attributes |
|-------|---------|------------|
| Name | `<input type="text" id="contact-name" name="name">` | `required autocomplete="name" maxlength="100"` |
| Email | `<input type="email" id="contact-email" name="email">` | `required autocomplete="email" inputmode="email" maxlength="254" spellcheck="false"` |
| Message | `<textarea id="contact-message" name="message" rows="6">` | `required minlength` **not** set (our own message is shown, not the browser's) · `maxlength="5000"` · `aria-describedby="contact-message-hint"` |

`field` class constant (inputs and textarea):
`block w-full rounded-none border-0 border-b-2 border-ink-muted bg-surface-alt px-4 py-3 text-base text-ink aria-[invalid=true]:border-danger focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline-accent`
- Inputs add `h-12`. The textarea adds `min-h-40 resize-y`.
- `aria-invalid` is written **only after the first submit attempt** `[CONTEXT D-10]`. Note `aria-[invalid=true]:`: Tailwind 4 has no built-in `aria-invalid` variant. `[Inference: Tailwind's built-in aria variants are busy/checked/disabled/expanded/hidden/pressed/readonly/required/selected; confirm in the built CSS]`

```
  <p id="contact-summary-title" class="… text-danger"><IconAlertCircle size-5/> {contact.summary.title}</p>
```

### Spam guard (visible parts) `[CONTEXT D-13, D-14; Research: Pattern 4, Pitfall 11]`

**Honeypot:**
```
<div aria-hidden="true" class="absolute -left-[9999px]">
  <label for="contact-extra">{contact.honeypot}</label>
  <input id="contact-extra" name="extra_info" type="text" tabindex="-1" autocomplete="off">
```

- Off-screen, not `display:none` (bots skip those). Not reachable by Tab. A human who somehow reaches it reads "Leave this field empty". - A honeypot hit gets **the same generic failure as any other failed send** (failure block below), never a fake success. A human false positive still gets Retry and the prefilled mailto. `[CONTEXT D-14 discretion → Research: Pitfall 11 recommendation]`
- **No minimum time-to-submit check in v1.** `[Research: Open Question 2 recommendation; Default]`

**Turnstile slot (inside the submit row, before the button):**
```
<div data-turnstile class="min-h-[140px] w-[150px]">
  <p class="text-sm text-ink-muted">{contact.turnstile.loading}</p>   ← replaced by the widget
</div>
<p data-turnstile-note hidden class="text-sm …"></p>                   ← wait / load-error notes, under the row
```
- Explicit render: `size: 'compact'`, `theme: 'light'`, `language: locale`, `action: 'contact'`. Never the implicit `class="cf-turnstile"`. `[Research: Pattern 4]`
- **When the script loads** `[Research: Open Question 1 → decided here]`: when `#contact` comes within 600px of the viewport (IntersectionObserver `rootMargin: '600px'`), **or** immediately when the URL hash is `#contact` or a `?service=` param is present, **or** on the first `focusin` inside the form, whichever comes first. It is injected once (guarded against a second injection by a later `<ClientRouter />`). A no-JS page makes no request to Cloudflare.

- **`error-callback`:** show `contact.turnstile.error` in the note (danger + alert icon), announce it, and keep the form usable for the email fallback.

### Submit button and states `[CONTEXT D-11, D-14, D-15]`

`<button type="submit" class={primaryButton}>` with a label `<span>` plus a trailing icon slot.

| State | Label | Icon | Behaviour |
|-------|-------|------|-----------|
| Ready | "Send message" | `arrow-right` | Validates on the client; if valid, sends |
| Sending | "Sending…" | `loader-2`, `motion-safe:animate-spin` (static under reduced motion) | `aria-disabled="true"`, `aria-disabled:cursor-progress`. Further submits are ignored by the handler. The status region says "Sending your message…" |

## Accessibility Contract (Phase 5 acceptance)

- Information is never conveyed by colour alone: errors have text + icon; the checked chip has a fill + icon; links are underlined on hover/focus and, in the summary, at rest.
