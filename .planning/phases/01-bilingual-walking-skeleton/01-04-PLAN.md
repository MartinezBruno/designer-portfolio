## Phase Goal

**As a** visitor (a potential client), **I want to** open Martina's site in English at `/` or Spanish at `/es/`, switch languages, and see her hero immediately, **so that** I can get a first impression of her work in my language.

<objective>
SEO + localized-404 slice: every page emits correct language/canonical/hreflang/OG metadata, and a visitor who hits a missing URL sees a localized 404 with a way home (bilingual at the root, because `astro preview` and most static hosts serve only the root 404.html).

Purpose: Delivers I18N-04 and I18N-07. Introduces BaseLayout `kind: 'page' | 'notFound'` so 404s opt out of canonical/hreflang, add noindex, and point the toggle at the other home.
Output: seo.ts + SeoHead, NotFoundView, 404 pages, switcher 404 behavior, unit + e2e tests.
</objective>
