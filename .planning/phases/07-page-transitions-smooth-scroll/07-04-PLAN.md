## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 7 goal (verbatim): "Moving through the site feels fluid: animated page transitions, inertial scrolling, and parallax. Keyboard users, screen-reader users, and find-in-page all keep working, and animations never leak between pages."

<objective>
Vertical slice 4: arrival feels like a fresh load. After the wipe uncovers the page, the Phase 6 entrances play (not hidden under the ink), focus lands at that moment, back/forward returns to the same scroll position with the right reveal state, and a live toggle or rapid clicking never strands the page mid-transition.

Purpose: MOTN-05 arrival quality (D-14 timing, D-15, D-16, D-18) and MOTN-09 robustness (aborted transitions leave no attributes behind).
Output: release gate in navigation.ts, delayed mount in lifecycle.ts, hero hold CSS, restored-position reveal/hero rules, e2e for back restore, timing, flash limit and live toggle, capture item 6.
</objective>
