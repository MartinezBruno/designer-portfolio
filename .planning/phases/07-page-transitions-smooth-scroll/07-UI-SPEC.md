# Phase 7 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**What this contract covers:** how moving through the site looks and behaves on Home, Projects, project detail and 404 pages, in EN and ES:
- the ink panel wipe on every same-site navigation, including the EN/ES switch (MOTN-05, D-01, D-03)
- the card title → detail `<h1>` morph (D-02)
- the persistent header and cursor during a transition (D-05)
- the `reduced` crossfade and the `off` full page load (D-04, SC4)
- arrival: focus, title, announcement, the Phase 6 entrance after the wipe, back/forward (D-14..D-16)
- smooth scroll on fine pointers, anchor glides, the skip link, keyboard and find-in-page (MOTN-06, D-06..D-08)
- parallax on project covers and the Next-project thumbnail (D-09, D-10, D-12, D-13), and a scroll-linked side entrance for the detail image stack (D-11a, revised 2026-11-02; it replaces the detail-stack drift of D-11)
- live behaviour when the motion toggle changes (D-18), the leak contract (MOTN-09, D-17) and the bundle record

**Out of scope:** WebGL or displacement effects (MOTN-10, v2), the measured Lighthouse budget and the full axe/keyboard pass (Phase 8), a reverse morph from detail back to a card (see Open Questions), parallax on the hero portrait (D-09).

**Inheritance:** `01-UI-SPEC.md` through `06-UI-SPEC.md` (all approved) still apply where this document is silent. That covers the colour tokens, the type scale, the 4px spacing scale, the accent focus ring, 44px touch targets, the pill and text-link patterns, the stretched-link card, the three motion states, the motion tokens, the reveal vocabulary, the hero sequence, the card mat/zoom hover, the cursor follower, magnetic targets and the motion toggle. **Phase 7 adds no colour, no type size, no spacing token and no copy string.** It adds three motion tokens, two parallax tokens, one detail-stack entrance token (`--stack-travel`, added 2026-11-02 for D-11a) and the transition choreography.

| Behaviour | `on` | `reduced` | `off` |
|-----------|------|-----------|-------|
| Same-site navigation | Soft navigation with the ink wipe | Soft navigation with a root opacity crossfade | **Full page load** (the router is opted out in `astro:before-preparation`) `[Research: Pitfall 9]` |
| Card → detail title morph | yes | no (no names assigned) | no |
| Header | still (own VT group, no animation) | still | n/a (full load) |
| Cursor follower | persists, live, above the wipe | not mounted (Phase 6) | not mounted |
| Lenis smooth wheel | fine pointer only | no | no |
| Content anchor glide | yes (Lenis on fine pointer, native smooth on touch) | instant jump | instant jump |
| Skip link | instant jump, always | instant jump | instant jump |
| Parallax | yes, touch included | no, `translate: none`, no overscan | no |
| Detail-stack side entrance (D-11a) | yes, touch included, scrubbed by scroll position | no, `translate: none` (exact Phase 4/6 box) | no |
| Focus to new h1 after navigation | yes | yes | n/a (full load starts at the top of the document) |
| Phase 6 entrances after arrival | full path, after the wipe releases | fade path, after the crossfade | static |

**`data-motion` survives every swap.** In `astro:before-swap` (app scope, registered once), the live `html[data-motion]` is copied onto `event.newDocument.documentElement`, and the new document's `[data-motion-toggle]` gets the current `aria-pressed`, so neither the motion state nor the toggle icon flickers. This lands before any other Phase 7 work. `[Research: Pattern 1, Pitfall 1, Pitfall 2; CONTEXT D-05]`

**Live switching (D-18), no reload:**

| On `motion:change` to… | Effect |
|------------------------|--------|
| `reduced` | Lenis `destroy()`s at once (native scroll from that frame, no jump: Lenis already scrolls the real document). Parallax stops through CSS (`[data-motion='on']` selectors stop matching) and images return to `translate: none`. The next navigation uses the crossfade. A transition already running is finished with `skipTransition()`. |
| `on` | Lenis is created at the current scroll position (fine pointer only). Parallax resumes from CSS at the current position. The next navigation uses the wipe. Nothing replays. |

Easing: the panel and the morph use **`--ease-travel`** (`cubic-bezier(0.76, 0, 0.24, 1)`), the Phase 6 shutter curve: they are things that travel while visible. Parallax is `linear` (scroll-linked). The detail-stack side entrance is an entrance, so its scrubbed keyframe uses **`--ease-reveal`** (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, the project's ease-out). Nothing in this phase uses bounce, elastic or back. The CSS keywords `ease-in` and `ease-in-out` must not appear anywhere in source, including comments, because `lint.mjs` flags the substring. Use the tokens. `[Taste: motion.md §2 question 3; lint ease-in]`

**Check on the parallax numbers** `[Verified: arithmetic]`: cards image height = 108% of the frame; 3.5% of 108 = 3.78% of the frame, below the 4% overscan, so an image edge never enters the frame. Total travel ≈ 7.6% of the frame. ~~Detail stack: image = 106% of the frame; 2.6% of 106 = 2.76%, below 3%. Total travel ≈ 5.5%.~~ (superseded 2026-11-02). **Check on the stack entrance (D-11a)** `[Verified: arithmetic]`: the image is 100% of its frame, with no overscan. It starts offset by 7% of its width toward its entry side and ends at 0. That is a single one-way travel of 7%, inside D-10's "about 5–10%" and under the skill's "under 8% of the element" for scroll-linked image moves. At its peak, a 7%-wide `surface-alt` strip shows on the trailing side of the frame and 7% of the image is clipped on the leading side, but only while the frame is entering. At rest, nothing is cropped. Concrete widths: a full-bleed image at 1440 starts about 101 px off, a padded `max-w-6xl` image about 76 px off, and a 390 px phone image about 27 px off. That reads clearly as "from the side" on desktop and stays quiet on a phone. **Moderate is the ceiling:** the taste review may lower the value (not below 5%) if it reads loud, but it never raises it. A broken seam is fixed with an opt-out, never with more travel. `[CONTEXT D-10, D-11a; Taste: motion.md "Scroll-linked progress"]` `[CONTEXT D-10; Taste: motion.md "Scroll-linked progress"]`

## Navigation Transition: the Ink Wipe (`on`) `[CONTEXT D-01, D-03, D-05]`

### The panel

- One element in `BaseLayout.astro`, outside `<main>`: `div[data-wipe-panel][aria-hidden="true"]`, `position: fixed; inset: 0; background: var(--color-ink); pointer-events: none`, parked off-screen at rest (`translate: 0 -100%`). It has no text, no border, no radius, no shadow and no gradient. `[Taste: house rules]`
- Under `html[data-motion='on']` it carries `view-transition-name: wipe`. In `reduced` and `off` it has no name and never moves.

### Direction `[Default inside CONTEXT discretion]`

The panel travels **vertically**. Vertical keeps it in the family of the Phase 6 vocabulary, where everything rises or opens along the vertical axis (line rise, settle, shutter). It also uncovers the top of the arriving page first, which is where every page's h1 sits.

| Navigation | Panel path | Reads as |
|------------|-----------|----------|
| Link click, EN/ES switch, forward traverse | enters from the **top** edge, covers top-down, exits through the **bottom** (`translate: 0 -100%` → `0 100%`) | a curtain dropping past the page |
| Back traverse (`html[data-astro-transition="back"]`) | enters from the **bottom**, exits through the **top** (`0 100%` → `0 -100%`) | the same curtain rewinding |

The EN/ES switch uses the forward path, even on the same logical page. `[CONTEXT D-03]`

### Timeline (one pass, `--t-wipe` = ~~560ms~~ 480ms, `--ease-travel`)

**Amended 2026-11-04 (07-13, user decision at the 07-10 checkpoint):** `--t-wipe` is 480ms. The percentages and panel positions below are unchanged (same curve); only the times scale by 480/560. Old times are struck through.

Panel progress below is measured from the easing curve. `[Verified: numeric cubic-bezier evaluation, 2026-11-02]`

| Time | Panel (forward) | Old page (`::view-transition-old(root)`) | New page (`::view-transition-new(root)`) |
|------|-----------------|-------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| 0ms | above the viewport | fully visible, still | hidden (`opacity: 0`) |
| ~~140ms~~ 120ms (25%) | leading edge at ~11% of the viewport | covered top 11% | hidden |
| ~~280ms~~ 240ms (50%) | covers 100% | **hidden by a step at 50%** (fully covered at that instant) | **shown by a step at 50%** |
| ~~336ms~~ 288ms (60%) | top ~60% uncovered | — | top 60% visible (live) |
| **~~392ms~~ 336ms (70%)** | top ~83% uncovered | — | **entrances released** (see Arrival) |
| ~~560ms~~ 480ms | below the viewport | — | fully visible |

- Old and new roots **never crossfade under the ink**: the swap happens in one step while the panel fully covers the content. Their animations are `opacity` steps (`steps(1, jump-end)` or equivalent keyframes at 50%), with no default UA crossfade. `[Default; Taste: nothing fades into anything]`
- The back traverse mirrors the panel path. The roots still switch at 50%. The release stays at 70%.

### Stacking during a transition (bottom → top)

1. `root` (old / new page)
2. `wipe` (the panel)
3. `site-header`: the header has `view-transition-name: site-header` in `on` **and** `reduced`, `animation: none` on its group and images, with the old image hidden and the new image shown at once. So the header never moves or fades. On the EN/ES switch its labels and `aria-current` change instantly at the start. `[CONTEXT D-05; Research: Anti-pattern "transition:persist on the header"]`
4. `project-title` (only on a card → detail navigation)
5. `cursor`: the follower layer carries `view-transition-name: cursor` with no animation, so the live dot keeps following the pointer above the ink. `[Proposed; Inference: pointer events keep reaching document listeners during a view transition. Check in the Wave 0 spike]`

The header is opaque `bg-surface` and `sticky top-0`, so the panel shows only below it. The header's existing bottom border stays as is (accepted `hairline` WARN since Phase 3). `[Codebase: SiteHeader.astro]`

### What the visitor's pointer sees

- On navigation start the "View" pill hides (`--t-press`) and the dot returns to its rest state, then the dot follows live above the ink. `[Default]`
- The panel never takes clicks (`pointer-events: none`). A click during a running transition starts a new navigation, which is the router's documented abort path. Names from the aborted transition are cleared. `[Research: Pitfall 8]`

### Loading feedback

There is no progress bar, spinner or preloader. Hover prefetch (the router default) normally makes the fetch near-instant on fine pointers. `[Default; Upstream: Phase 6 D-03 no preloader]` `[Unverified]` On touch there is no hover, so a navigation may wait for the network before the wipe starts. The executor records the median click-to-wipe-start time on a throttled "Fast 4G" Pixel 7 profile in the SUMMARY. If it is over 300ms, it is raised with the user, not hidden.

**Amended 2026-11-04 (07-16, closes the 07-10 touch latency follow-up).** 07-10 measured a 538.5 ms cold-cache median from tap to wipe start on the throttled Pixel 7. Two changes answer it:

- **Pressed state.** On `astro:before-preparation` (the router has accepted a soft navigation and has not fetched yet), `navigation.ts` sets the constant attribute `data-nav-pressed` on `event.sourceElement.closest('a')`.
  - **Clearing.** The marker is removed on `astro:before-swap`, when the next navigation starts, after a 3000 ms safety timeout, and on unbind. It never reaches the arriving page.
  - **Look.** It reuses the hover vocabulary: a link takes the 2px accent underline (offset 4px) in ink text, and a card or the Next-project teaser takes the Phase 6 mat inset (`clip-path: inset(12px)`). There is no spinner, bar, new colour or token, and no layout shift.
  - **Per motion state.** Under `on`, the mat rides its existing rest transition. A card title's own underline is not drawn while the 07-06 morph wrapper holds the title (the decoration does not reach the inline-block wrapper, as in the Title Morph Colour row), so the mat is the card's cue. Under `reduced`, the mat has no transition: the inset lands at once, and no transform, translate or scale changes. Under `off`, nothing is marked (it is a full page load).
- **Latency profiles** (Pixel 7, CDP 150 ms latency, 1.6 Mbps down; 10 taps on the Projects nav link from Home, median):

| Profile | Tap -> `data-nav-pressed` | Tap -> `startViewTransition` |
|---------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| cold (HTTP cache disabled, uncompressed `astro preview`) | 1 ms | 537.5 ms |
| default (browser cache and touchstart prefetch) | 1 ms | 297.5 ms |
| cold-gzip (cache disabled, gzip proxy for HTML/JS/CSS; `/projects/` 6,511 B on the wire instead of 28,519 B) | 1 ms | 218.5 ms |

The pressed column measures when the attribute is set in the DOM, not when it is painted, which happens one frame later. The cold-gzip profile approximates Cloudflare's edge compression with a local proxy `[Unverified: the deployed edge is not measured; the site is not deployed]`. The approximated production median is under 300 ms and the tap is acknowledged at once, so the 07-10 follow-up is **resolved**.

## Title Morph (card → detail, `on` only) `[CONTEXT D-02; Research: Pattern 4]`

**Sources:** the clicked card's title in Home `compact` cards, Projects `full` cards and `NextProjectTeaser` (its h3 title). **Target:** the detail intro `<h1>`.

| Rule | Contract |
|------|----------|
| What gets the name | A `display: inline-block; width: fit-content` span inside the clicked title link, never the stretched `<a>` (its `::after` covers the whole tile). Assigned in `astro:before-preparation` from `event.sourceElement`, only when it is inside `[data-card]` or the teaser |
| Arriving name | The same name (`project-title`) on a `fit-content` span inside the new document's h1, set in `astro:before-swap` |
| Uniqueness | Exactly one element per document carries `project-title`. ## `reduced` and `off` Navigation `[CONTEXT D-04; Upstream: 06 D-14]`

- **`reduced`:** no `wipe` or `project-title` names are assigned. `::view-transition-old(root)` fades out and `::view-transition-new(root)` fades in over `--t-nav-fade` (320ms), with `--ease-reveal`. The header stays still (its `site-header` name). No travel, no scale, no clip. The arriving page's Phase 6 fade-path entrances start when the crossfade finishes.
- **OS reduced and toggle reduced behave identically**, because no `transition:*` directive is used (no injected `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` override). `[Research: Pattern 3]`
- **`off`:** `astro:before-preparation` calls `preventDefault()`, and the router does a plain `location.href` load. The page is static, as in Phase 1. `[Research: Pitfall 9]`
- Browsers without same-document View Transitions get `fallback="swap"`: an instant swap with the same focus, title and announcement contract. `[Research: Summary]`

## Arrival: Focus, Title, Entrance, Back/Forward `[CONTEXT D-14..D-16]`

### Focus and announcement

| Case | Focus target | Scroll |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Soft navigation, no hash | the arriving page's `main h1` (`tabindex="-1"`), `focus({ preventScroll: true })` | top (router) |
| Soft navigation with a hash that resolves (for example `/projects/` → `/#contact`) | the hash target (`tabindex="-1"`), not the h1 | **instant** landing at the target after the wipe, respecting scroll padding. No glide after a wipe `[Default: Research Open Question 3]` |
| Back / forward | the h1, with `preventScroll`, so the restored position is untouched | restored by the router |
| Pages without an h1 | `#main` (already `tabindex="-1"`) | — |
| First load | nothing is focused by script | native |

- Every view's h1 gets `tabindex="-1"`: `Hero.astro`, `ProjectsView.astro`, `ProjectIntro.astro`, `NotFoundView.astro`. The planner confirms no other view renders a page. `[Codebase: CONTEXT code_context]`
- **No visible ring on programmatic focus:** `h1[tabindex='-1']:focus { outline: none }` (and the same for `#main`). These targets are not interactive; the next Tab moves into the page's content in DOM order. `[CONTEXT D-14]`
- **Timing:** focus moves when the entrances are released (at 70% of the wipe, or when the crossfade ends), never earlier. Moving focus earlier would make a screen reader read content that is still covered.
- **Title and announcement:** `document.title`, `<html lang>`, canonical and hreflang update with the swap `[Research: Verified probe]`. Astro's route announcer (`aria-live="assertive"`, the new title) stays. The h1 focus also reads the page name. Possible double announcement is an Open Question for VoiceOver UAT; nothing is hidden by default.

### Entrance after the wipe (D-16)

- While a transition runs, `html[data-vt]` holds the arriving page: the Phase 6 hero keyframes are paused (`animation-play-state: paused`), and the page lifecycle has not mounted yet, so reveals stay in their armed/hidden start state. `[Research: Pattern 2, Pitfall 3]`
- **Release** at `--wipe-release` (70% of `--t-wipe` = ~~392ms~~ 336ms since 07-13 after the transition's animations start) or when the transition finishes, whichever comes first, capped at 1200ms after the swap so a stalled transition can never strand hidden content. Release removes `html[data-vt]`, mounts the page lifecycle and moves focus. The Phase 6 1.5s CSS failsafe still applies underneath. `[Default; Research: Pattern 2 (mount on finished) adjusted to 70% for the D-16 budget]`
- Why 70%: at that moment the panel has uncovered the top ~83% of the viewport (forward path), and every page's first screen starts at the top, so nothing visible starts animating under the ink.
- **Settled budget (click to last above-fold element at rest, prefetched):**

| Page | Wipe until release | Entrance after release | Total | Target |
|------|-------------------|------------------------|-------|--------|
| Projects | 392ms | h1 line rise 640ms + 80ms per extra line, intro settle at +140ms (560ms) → ~720ms | **~1.11s** | ≤ 1.2s |
| Detail | 392ms | metadata settle at +210ms + 70ms per group, 560ms → ~840ms | **~1.23s** | ≤ about 1.2s |
| Detail via morph | 392ms (title already landed) | same as detail | **~1.23s** | ≤ about 1.2s |

The Home hero keeps its full timing because D-16 says entrances play "as they do on a fresh load" and the hero is the signature moment. This overshoot of about 150ms is accepted up front and recorded in the SUMMARY with the measured number. If the user wants Home inside 1.2s, the lever is a shorter `--t-wipe` (480ms), never a shortened hero. `[Default; Inference: totals are arithmetic from the tokens, measured in the e2e budget check]`

### Back / forward (D-15)

- The router restores `history.state.scrollY`. The back wipe (reversed path) plays.
- Lenis is created after the restore, so it starts at the restored position with no jump `[Research: Pattern 6]`. Parallax is CSS, so it is correct at the restored position on the first frame.
- **Reveal state at a restored position:** elements whose top is above the restored viewport's bottom edge and that are not in the viewport are set to `data-revealed` instantly (no entrance replays above the visitor). Elements in the viewport play their entrance after release. Elements below stay armed. If the restored position is past the hero, the hero is marked `data-hero-played` and does not run. `[Default; extends the Phase 6 late-mount rule]`
- Safari's swipe-back gesture skips the router animation (`hasUAVisualTransition`). Release, focus and reveals still run. `[Research: Router Decision table]`

## Smooth Scroll (fine pointer, `on`) `[CONTEXT D-06..D-08; Research: Pattern 6]`

| Option | Value | Why |
|--------|-------|-----|
| `lerp` | **0.12** | Slightly firmer than Lenis's 0.1 default: a light glide that catches up within a few frames, so fast scrollers never feel lag. Tune only inside **0.10–0.14** during the taste review and record the final value `[Default inside D-06]` |
| `smoothWheel` | `true` | Wheel and trackpad only |
| `syncTouch` | `false` | Touch keeps native momentum `[CONTEXT D-07]` |
| `wheelMultiplier` | `1` | Same distance per notch as native |
| `anchors` | `false` | Avoids the router's own hash handling `[Research: Pitfall 4]` |
| `stopInertiaOnNavigate` | `true` | A click mid-glide never carries momentum into the next page |
| `autoRaf` | `true` | No GSAP ticker (no ScrollTrigger) |

- **Mount:** page scope, on lifecycle mount, only when `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` matches and the state is `on`. Destroyed on unmount (`astro:before-swap`) and on `motion:change` away from `on`. It is never created on touch, in `reduced`, in `off`, or under `forced-colors: active`. `[Default for forced colours, matching the Phase 6 cursor]`
- **Never** set `scroll-behavior: smooth` globally: the router restores history with `scrollTo`, which would glide back from 0. `[Research: Pattern 5]`
- The four Lenis base rules are copied into `global.css` (`html.lenis { height: auto }`, `.lenis-stopped { overflow: clip }`, `[data-lenis-prevent] { overscroll-behavior: contain }`, `.lenis.lenis-smooth iframe { pointer-events: none }`), not imported from the lazy chunk.
- **Nested scrollers:** any element with its own scroll (`overflow: auto/scroll`) gets `data-lenis-prevent`. The planner greps for them. If none exist, the rule ships unused.
- **Embeds (discretion):** the Phase 4 click-to-load `EmbedFacade` stays. While Lenis is gliding, iframes ignore the pointer (the base rule above), so a glide never stalls over an embed. When idle, a loaded Figma iframe takes the wheel natively, as it would without Lenis. No `data-lenis-prevent` on embeds. `[Research: Pattern 6; Assumption A5: checked manually on a detail page with an embed — the page must never feel stuck or swallow the wheel]`

### Keyboard, find-in-page, scrollbar

- Arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Space, Shift+Space, Home and End scroll natively. Lenis does not intercept keys, and Tab order is unchanged. `[CONTEXT D-08]`
- Find-in-page: the browser scrolls to the match and Lenis must not pull back (it syncs to native scroll when it is not gliding). Dragging the scrollbar is native.

### Anchors and the skip link

| Link | `on` | `reduced` / `off` | Focus |
|------|------|-------------------|-------|
| Skip link (`data-skip-link`, `#main`) | **instant jump**, always | instant | `#main` at once |
| Same-page content anchor (for example the header "Contact" or the hero CTA on Home → `#contact`) | **glide** to the target, stopping at the scroll padding. Fine pointer: Lenis's glide, about 0.9–1.2s depending on distance. Touch: the browser's native smooth scroll | instant jump | the target (`tabindex="-1"`, `preventScroll`) **when the glide ends** (`scrollend`) |
| Cross-page link with a hash | wipe, then instant landing at the target | crossfade, then instant | the target |
| Home "Ask about this" service links (`/?service=<id>#contact`, same pathname as the current page) | treated as a same-page content anchor: **no wipe and no page swap**, glide to `#contact`, the matching service chip is checked, a half-typed message survives | instant jump, same chip and message behaviour | `#contact` |

- Anchor target sections get `tabindex="-1"` with the same no-ring rule as the h1.
- The "Ask about this" row is a contract on the outcome. Wiping Home onto itself would discard a typed message and replay the hero. The planner picks the mechanism (for example letting the router treat it as a same-page hash plus a chip update from the link's `service` param, or opting that link out of soft navigation and handling the scroll locally) and must not hand-roll `pushState` against the router's history state. `[Default; Research: Pitfall 4; Codebase: FeaturedServices.astro, service-preselect.ts]`

### Layering (one property per layer, extends the Phase 6 table) `[CONTEXT D-13]`

| Layer | Element | Owns |
|-------|---------|------|
| Frame | aspect div | reveal `clip-path`, reveal opacity, **`view-timeline-name`**, `overflow: clip` |
| Mat | `<picture data-card-mat>` | hover `clip-path`, reveal `scale` |
| Image | `<img>` | hover `scale` (transition) **and parallax `translate` (scroll-linked animation)** |

`translate` and `scale` are independent CSS properties and compose, so the hover zoom, the reveal shutter and the parallax never overwrite each other. The mat's 12px hover inset stays even on all four sides because the mat itself does not move. `[Research: Pattern 7; Upstream: 06 card layering]`

### Mechanics

- Under `html[data-motion='on']` and `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`: the frame sets a named `view-timeline` (block axis). The image is sized to `100% + 2 × --px-overscan` of the frame's height, offset upward by one overscan, `object-fit: cover`, and animated `from { translate: 0 calc(var(--px-travel) * -1) } to { translate: 0 var(--px-travel) }`, `linear`, `animation-range: cover 0% cover 100%`, `fill: both`.
- A named timeline on the frame is required: `view()` on an image inside an `overflow: hidden` frame stays stuck, because `hidden` makes the frame a scroll container. `overflow: clip` does not. `[Research: Pitfall 5, Verified in Chromium]`
- Overscan exists **only** under `on`. In `reduced`, `off`, without support, or after a live toggle to `reduced`, the image returns to exactly the Phase 6 box (no crop, `translate: none`). That visible re-crop of at most 4% per edge on a toggle is accepted. `[Default]`
- No layout shift: the frame's box never changes in any state. Only the image moves inside the clip. `[CONTEXT D-10]`
- Lenis scrolls the real document, so the timelines follow its glide. `[Assumption A7: no visible stepping; checked in the taste review at 1440 on a 60Hz and a 120Hz display if available]`

### Detail-stack side entrance (`on`, all pointers) `[CONTEXT D-11a, revised 2026-11-02; replaces the D-11 drift]`

| Target | Frame (owns the timeline and the clip) | Moving layer | Token |
|--------|----------------------------------------|--------------|-------|
| Eligible detail `ImageModule` images | a **new** frame per eligible image: `data-stack-frame`, `bg-surface-alt overflow-clip`, inline `aspect-ratio: <width> / <height>` from the image's own metadata, so the stack keeps its exact height and stays seamless | the `<img>`, carrying `data-stack-enter="left"` or `"right"` | `--stack-travel` 7% |

- **Eligible:** a detail `ImageModule` image that is not the eager (LCP) image, whose project is not opted out, and whose image id is not listed in the opt-out. Never moving: the eager image, `CollectionModule` grids, `EmbedFacade`, text, unknown modules, and anything in `reduced`, `off`, print or browsers without scroll timelines. Images that never move render exactly the Phase 4/6 markup, with no extra frame.
- **Alternation rule:** the direction counts **eligible images only**, in DOM order across the whole page. It starts at `left`, and it does **not** reset after a `CollectionModule`, text or embed. Static modules are transparent to the count, so the moving images always read left, right, left... with no two neighbours entering from the same side, even when an opted-out or eager image sits between them. `[Proposed; recorded in 07-09]`
- **Mechanics:** under `html[data-motion='on']` and `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`, the frame sets `view-timeline: --stack block`. The image runs `@keyframes stack-enter` from `translate: calc(var(--stack-from) * var(--stack-travel)) 0` to `translate: 0 0`, with `--stack-from: -1` for `left` and `1` for `right`, `animation-timing-function: var(--ease-reveal)`, `fill: both`, `animation-timeline: --stack` (declared **after** the `animation` shorthand, which would otherwise reset it), and `animation-range: entry 0% entry 100%`. The image is fully settled once the frame has fully entered the viewport, or, for a frame taller than the viewport, once its top reaches the viewport top. The taste review may end the range earlier (not before `entry 60%`), never later.
- **Scroll-linked, never timed:** the position depends only on scroll position. There is no IntersectionObserver, no class toggle, no lifecycle registration, and nothing to leak. During the 07-04 arrival hold (`html[data-vt]`) and under the wipe, each image simply sits at the state its scroll position gives it, so nothing "plays" under the ink and release causes no jump. Back/forward restores scroll first, so images are correct on the first frame (D-15).
- **No interference with the morph:** frames and images never carry a `view-transition-name`. Only the clicked card's title and the h1 take part in the 07-06 morph. The first image under the intro is the eager image, which never moves.
- **No layout shift, no page overflow:** the frame's box never changes in any state. `overflow: clip` keeps the horizontal travel inside the frame, so the document never gains horizontal scroll.
- **Layering:** detail images have no hover, mat or reveal shutter, so `translate` is the only animated property on the `<img>`. It does not collide with card parallax (a different attribute, a different keyframe name and a different timeline name).
- **Print:** `[data-stack-enter]` gets `animation: none !important; translate: none !important`.

### Seam rule for the detail stack (D-11a, keeps the D-11 spirit)

- While a frame is entering, its image is offset sideways against the settled image above it, so a composition that continues across that seam is visibly broken **during the slide**. It is whole again at rest. That is the effect the user asked for, and it is acceptable for images that each stand alone.
- The taste review checks **InstaBlogs** and **Cabrera's** (and any project whose images continue one composition across a seam, judged from a full-page screenshot) at 1440 and 390, at three scroll positions per seam: entering (the lower frame's top just inside the viewport), mid (about half of its entry range) and settled (range end). If a horizontal slide visibly breaks a composition that continues across a seam (for example one long website mockup sliced into several images), **the side entrance is removed for those images** (preferred: list the image ids; whole project with `stackMotion: false` only if most seams break). It is never compensated by raising travel. The opt-out lives in the local overrides data (`stackMotion` in `src/data/project-overrides.json`), not in component code. `[CONTEXT D-11a]`
- Images that carry text near the left or right edge are checked at the entering position. Text that is clipped mid-slide and readable at rest is accepted. Text that is still cropped at rest is a bug, because the image must rest at 0.

## Accessibility Contract (Phase 7 acceptance)

- **SC1:** after every soft navigation in `on` and `reduced`, `document.activeElement` is the arriving `main h1` (or the hash target, or `#main`), `document.title` matches the page, and `<html lang>`, canonical and hreflang (en, es, x-default) are correct, including after EN/ES switches. - **SC2:** in `on` with a fine pointer, PageDown, Space, End and Home change `scrollY`; Tab order is unchanged from Phase 6 (`expectedOrderFor`); the `#contact` anchor glides, sets the hash and focuses the target after `scrollend`; the skip link focuses `#main` instantly; `window.find('<text>')` (Chromium proxy for find-in-page) scrolls to the match and `scrollY` is stable 500ms later. The real find bar is a manual UAT step in Chrome, Safari and Firefox.
- **SC3:** the leak contract above.
- **SC4:** with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` and with the toggle pressed: no `wipe` or `project-title` pseudo-element animations (root crossfade only), no `html.lenis`, computed `translate` on every `[data-parallax]` and every `[data-stack-enter]` is `none`, and switching the toggle live (no reload) destroys Lenis and stops parallax. In `off` (`matchMedia` stubbed out), a navigation creates a new `navigation` performance entry (full load).
- **No hidden content:** with JS disabled, nothing in this phase applies (no panel name, no overscan, no Lenis). With the motion chunk blocked after the gate, the Phase 6 failsafe still shows everything by 1.6s, and a soft navigation that never releases is released by the 1200ms cap.
- **Vestibular:** the panel is a single flat pass of ~~560ms~~ 480ms (07-13); it never flashes: one dark field per navigation. `[Inference]` Rapid clicking cannot exceed three flashes per second (WCAG 2.3.1), because a new navigation aborts the running one; the e2e clicks five links within one second and counts wipe animations to confirm.
