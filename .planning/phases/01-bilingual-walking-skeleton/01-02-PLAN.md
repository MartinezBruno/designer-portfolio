## Phase Goal

**As a** visitor (a potential client), **I want to** open Martina's site in English at `/` or Spanish at `/es/`, switch languages, and see her hero immediately, **so that** I can get a first impression of her work in my language.

<objective>
Language-switching slice: a visitor can move between `/` and `/es/` with the "EN / ES" header toggle, is never redirected by browser language, and can skip to content. All UI-SPEC strings exist in typed EN/ES dictionaries where a missing Spanish key is a compile error.

Purpose: Delivers I18N-02/03/05 and the header + skip-link half of I18N-06. The complete dictionary lands here so later slices (hero, footer, SEO, 404) only consume keys and never edit the dictionary files concurrently.
Output: Dictionaries, `t()`/`interpolate()`, locale route helpers, SkipLink, LanguageSwitcher, SiteHeader, BaseLayout wiring, unit + e2e tests.
</objective>
